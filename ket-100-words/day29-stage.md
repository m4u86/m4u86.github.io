## 教学目标清单（Target Outcomes）
1. **写作提分**：熟练运用 100 词中的高级形容词与句型，替换简单词（如用 `fantastic / awesome` 替换 good，用 `pleased with` 替换 happy）。
2. **便条模板（Part 6）**：掌握 35 词邀请信/道歉信/感谢信的三段式结构：问候 + 主题（理由/安排）+ 结尾期待。
3. **口语应答（Speaking Part 1 & 2）**：在描述个人爱好、假期活动与朋友性格时，自然融入 3~4 个高阶词汇。

## Scene 2: 35-Word Note Writing Workshop（35词便条满分工作坊，6 min）

### KET 写作真题情境：
> **Task**: You are having a party next Saturday. Write an email to your English friend, Alex:
> 1. Invite Alex to your party.
> 2. Tell Alex when and where the party will be.
> 3. Tell Alex what to bring or wear.
> *(Write 25-35 words)*

### 📝 满分范文展示（标注本课程核心词）：
> *Dear Alex,*  
> *I would like to **invite** you to my birthday party next Saturday! It starts at 6 PM at my house. We **plan** to play **awesome** video games and eat cake. Please wear **comfortable** clothes!*  
> *See you soon,*  
> *Bob*  
> *(36 words)*

**【得分点剖析】**：
- 准确回应了全部 3 个要点；
- 巧妙使用了 `invite`, `plan`, `awesome`, `comfortable` 4 个 KET 高频核心词汇；
- 语法准确，拼写零差错，字数在 35 词左右。

## Scene 3: Speaking Part 1 Simulation（口语考官模拟问答，5 min）

**[Ms. Emma (考官)]**：“*What kind of music do you like listening to?*”  
**[Alex (学员应答 - 基础版)]**：“*I like pop music because it is good and I like it.*” (得分偏低，词汇单薄)  
**[Alex (学员应答 - 提分升级版)]**：  
> *“I enjoy listening to pop music because it is very **relaxing** after a **dull** day at school. It's **especially** enjoyable when I listen to it with my friends!”*

**【加分词亮点】**：融入了 `relaxing`, `dull`, `especially`, `enjoyable`，展现了极强的词汇丰富度（Lexical Resource）！
