## Scene 2: High-Yield Masterclass & Pitfall Radar（核心考点与排雷，5 min）

### 考点 1：KET 听力经典“看图辨物”圈套（striped vs spotted）
- 听力常考：听一段对话选出丢失的书包/狗/外套。
  - `striped`：由一条条线条组成（The striped shirt is my favorite）。
  - `spotted`：由斑点/波点组成（The spotted dog was hiding behind the tree）。
  - 拼写注意：`spotted` 必须**双写 t**（spot $\to$ spotted）。

### 考点 2：购物支付介词搭配（credit card vs cash）
- ❌ *pay with cash* (口语虽有，但 KET 标答更强调：`pay in cash` 或 `pay by cash`)
- ✅ `pay by credit card`（用信用卡支付）
- ✅ `pay in cash`（付现金）
  - 核心例句：*You can pay with a credit card, but do you have enough cash for the tickets?*

### 考点 3：赛事家族（match vs competition）
- `football / tennis match`：球类比赛通常用 `match`。
- `cooking / singing / dancing competition`：技能类大比拼用 `competition`（动词为 compete）。

## Scene 3: Deep Interactive / Micro-Quiz（真题微测，3 min）

#### 题目 1（KET 听力图片选词情境）：
> "The police officer is looking for a small dog with black circles on its white fur."  
> The dog is __________.  
> A. striped  
> B. spotted  
> C. comfortable  
> **【正确答案】** B  
> **【解析】** black circles（黑色圆圈/斑点）对应 spotted。

#### 题目 2（KET 标识阅读理解）：
> 🪧 **Sign on Door**: *“CASH ONLY. NO CHEQUES OR CARDS ACCEPTED.”*  
> What does the sign mean?  
> A. You can pay by credit card.  
> B. You must have enough cash to pay here.  
> C. Entry tickets are completely free today.  
> **【正确答案】** B  
> **【解析】** Cash only 说明只能付现金，不能刷卡。
