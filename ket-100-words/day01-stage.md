## Scene 2: High-Yield Masterclass & Pitfall Radar（核心考点与排雷，5 min）

**[Ms. Emma 主讲导师]**：“既然学过，我们不废话，直击 KET 阅卷老师最爱扣分的**三大隐形陷阱**！”

### 陷阱 1：主语搭配错位（-ed vs -ing）
- ❌ *The exam result was very disappointed.* (错！考试结果不能‘自己感到失望’)
- ✅ *The exam result was **disappointing**.* / *She was **disappointed** with the result.*
- 💡 **记忆口诀**：人感到失望用 `disappointed`，令人满意的结果用 `pleasing / satisfactory`，人感到满意用 `be pleased with`。
  - 例句：*He was **pleased with** his performance in the exam.*

### 陷阱 2：KET 听力拼写“吞音与漏字母”重灾区
- `comfortable`：很多孩子漏写中间的 `or`，写成 *comftable*。请记住拆分法：`comfort`（安慰/舒适）+ `able`（能够的）。
- `disappointed`：双写 `p` 还是单写 `s`？**单 s 双 p**（dis + ap + point + ed）！

### 陷阱 3：反义词与极高级同义替换
- `dull` = boring（枯燥乏味的）。
  - 例句：*The lecture was so **dull** that many students fell asleep.*
- `fabulous` & `amazing` = very good / wonderful（写作中替换 good 的加分词！）。
- `popular` 固定搭配：`be popular among / with teenagers`（受青少年欢迎）。

## Scene 3: Deep Interactive / Micro-Quiz（真题微测，3 min）

**[互动答题卡]**（学员在互动板上选择，智能体即时评分）：

#### 题目 1（KET 选词完形模拟）：
> After walking in the mountains for six hours, the warm bath at the hotel was extremely __________.  
> A. dull  
> B. relaxing  
> C. disappointed  
> **【正确答案】** B  
> **【解析】** 修饰 warm bath（物），且由常识可知登山6小时后泡温水澡是“令人放松的（relaxing）”。

#### 题目 2（KET 易混改错）：
> The new Harry Potter movie is very __________ with pupils all over the world.  
> A. popular  
> B. enjoyable  
> C. comfortable  
> **【正确答案】** A  
> **【解析】** `be popular with sb.` 为固定高频搭配，意为“受……欢迎”。
